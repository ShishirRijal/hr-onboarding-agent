# NordTech Solutions — Working Hours & Payroll Policy

## 1. Working Hours

**Section: Standard Working Week**

- Full-time employees work **40 hours per week**, Monday to Friday, in line with Estonian, Finnish, and Portuguese working-time law. Lunch breaks are not counted as working time.
- Working hours are flexible: you choose your own start and end times, as long as you cover core hours and your team commitments.
- Part-time employees agree their weekly hours and schedule individually with their manager; these are recorded in Personio and in the employment contract.

**Section: Core Working Hours**

- Core working hours are **10:00–15:00 in your local office time zone**, Monday to Friday. Tallinn and Tampere are on Eastern European Time; Porto is on Western European Time.
- During core hours you are expected to be reachable on Slack and available for meetings, whether you work from the office or from home.
- Fully remote employees working from another time zone agree their core hours with their manager in writing, and set them as working hours in Google Calendar so colleagues can see when they are available.

## 2. Overtime

**Section: Overtime**

- Overtime must be **agreed with your manager in advance**. Regularly working beyond 40 hours a week is not expected and should be raised with your manager as a workload issue.
- Approved overtime is logged in Personio and compensated in line with local law and your employment contract, either as time off in lieu or as overtime pay, as agreed with your manager.
- Unapproved extra hours are not compensated, except where local law requires it.

## 3. Payroll and Payslips

**Section: Pay Date**

- Salaries are paid **monthly on the 15th**. If the 15th falls on a weekend or a public holiday, salary is paid on the previous working day.
- Approved expense reimbursements are paid in the same payroll run (see the Expense & Reimbursement Policy).

**Section: Payroll Cutoff**

- Changes that affect pay — new bank details, approved expenses, unpaid leave, overtime — must be in Personio by the **5th of the month** to be included in that month's payroll. Anything recorded after the 5th rolls into the following month's run.

**Section: Your First Salary**

- Add your bank details and tax information in Personio during your first week (see the Onboarding Checklist).
- If you start on or before the 5th, your first salary is paid on the 15th of the same month. If you start after the 5th, your first partial month is paid together with the following month's salary.

**Section: Payslips**

- Payslips are published in Personio under **Documents > Payslips** on payday. They are not sent by email.
- For questions about your payslip, tax withholding, or a payment that looks wrong, contact Ott Rebane (Finance Manager), copying HR at hr@nordtech.ee.

## 4. Probation Period — All Countries

**Section: Probation Period**

- The 90-day probation review in the Onboarding Checklist is NordTech's internal check-in with your manager. Your **legal probation period** is set in your employment contract, within the statutory limits for your country of employment (Sections 5–7 below).
- Annual leave accrues from your first day and can be taken during probation, subject to manager approval (see the Leave & Absence Policy).

## 5. Estonia — Probation and Notice

**Section: Probation — Estonia**

- The probation period in Estonia is **up to 4 months**.
- During probation, either party can end the employment contract with at least **15 calendar days'** notice.

**Section: Resignation Notice — Estonia**

- After probation, employees who resign must give at least **30 calendar days'** written notice, unless their contract specifies a longer period.

## 6. Finland — Probation and Notice

**Section: Probation — Finland**

- The probation period in Finland is **up to 6 months**.
- During probation, either party can end the employment with immediate effect, provided the reason is not discriminatory or otherwise unrelated to the purpose of the probation period.

**Section: Resignation Notice — Finland**

- Employees who resign must give **14 days'** notice if they have been employed for 5 years or less, and **1 month's** notice if they have been employed for more than 5 years, unless their contract specifies otherwise.

## 7. Portugal — Probation and Notice

**Section: Probation — Portugal**

- The probation period in Portugal is **90 days** for most roles, up to **180 days** for technically complex or high-responsibility roles, and up to **240 days** for senior management roles. The applicable period is stated in your contract.
- During probation, either party can end the contract without cause. If probation has lasted more than 60 days, NordTech gives 7 days' notice; if it has lasted more than 120 days, 15 days' notice.

**Section: Resignation Notice — Portugal**

- Employees who resign must give **30 days'** written notice with less than 2 years of service, and **60 days'** notice with 2 or more years of service.

## 8. Further Questions

This policy does not cover salary levels, salary reviews, or bonuses — see the Employee Handbook (Section 4) and speak to your manager or HR. For anything not covered here, contact HR at hr@nordtech.ee rather than assuming a default.
