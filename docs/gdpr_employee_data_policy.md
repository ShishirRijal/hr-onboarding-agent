# NordTech Solutions — GDPR Employee Data Policy

*Document type: Legal / Compliance*
*Source doc ID: `gdpr_employee_policy`*
*Category: Legal*
*Country: All*
*Last updated: March 2026*

*Note: this document covers how NordTech collects, uses, retains, and protects its own employees' personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It does not cover the EU AI Act or NordTech's product-level AI compliance — see the EU AI Act Compliance Summary for that.*

---

## 1. What Personal Data NordTech Collects

**Section: Data Collected**

As your employer, NordTech collects and processes the following categories of personal data about you:

- **Identity and contact data**: full name, date of birth, home address, personal email, phone number.
- **Employment data**: job title, team, manager, employment contract terms, start date, and (where applicable) end date.
- **Financial data**: bank details for payroll, tax identification numbers, salary and compensation history.
- **Health data**: information relevant to health insurance enrollment (via Cigna) and sick leave records, collected only to the extent necessary to administer benefits and statutory sick pay.
- **Performance data**: performance review outcomes, self-assessments, peer feedback, and development plans (see the Employee Handbook, Section 4).
- **System and access data**: company account activity logs, device inventory, and security compliance status (see the IT Setup Guide).

## 2. Legal Basis for Processing

**Section: Legal Basis**

NordTech processes employee personal data under the following legal bases, depending on the category of data:

1. **Performance of the employment contract** — for data necessary to pay you, manage your role, and administer standard HR processes (e.g., payroll, leave records).
2. **Legal obligation** — for data NordTech is required to collect or retain under Estonian, Finnish, or Portuguese labour, tax, or social insurance law (e.g., statutory sick pay records, tax withholding data).
3. **Legitimate interest** — for limited purposes such as maintaining IT security logs or managing internal directories, balanced against employee privacy rights.
4. **Consent** — used only where no other legal basis applies, such as certain optional benefits enrollments; consent can be withdrawn at any time without affecting other aspects of employment.

## 3. Retention Periods

**Section: Retention Periods**

NordTech retains employee personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, in line with the following retention schedule:

- **Employment records** (contracts, role history, payroll records): retained for **7 years after the employee leaves** the company, to satisfy statutory and tax obligations across Estonia, Finland, and Portugal.
- **Health data** (insurance enrollment records, sick leave documentation): retained for **5 years**, after which it is securely deleted.
- **Recruitment data** for candidates who are **not hired**: retained for **6 months** after the recruitment process closes, after which it is deleted unless the candidate has separately consented to being considered for future roles.
- Data retained beyond these periods only occurs where required by an active legal dispute, audit, or ongoing statutory obligation in a specific country.

## 4. Employee Rights

**Section: Employee Rights**

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

1. **Access** — request a copy of the personal data NordTech holds about you.
2. **Rectification** — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
3. **Erasure** — request deletion of your personal data, subject to NordTech's legal retention obligations described in Section 3 (data cannot be deleted early if a statutory retention period still applies).
4. **Restriction** — request that NordTech limit how your data is processed in specific circumstances.
5. **Data portability** — request your data in a portable format where processing is based on consent or contract.
6. **Object** — object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, contact HR (hr@nordtech.ee), who will coordinate with NordTech's data protection function to respond within the statutory timeframe.

## 5. Data Breach Procedure

**Section: Data Breach Procedure**

If you discover or suspect a data breach — including a lost or stolen device, a phishing compromise, accidental disclosure of personal data, or unauthorized access to any system containing employee or customer data — you must:

1. **Report it to IT within 2 hours of discovery.** Use the #it-support Slack channel with "URGENT — possible data breach" in the message, or email it-support@nordtech.ee directly if Slack is unavailable.
2. Do not attempt to independently investigate, further access, or "fix" the affected system yourself — this can complicate the incident response and potential regulatory reporting timeline.
3. IT and Legal/Compliance will assess the breach and determine whether it requires notification to the relevant data protection authority (which, under GDPR, must generally happen within 72 hours of the organization becoming aware of the breach) and, where required, notification to affected individuals.

Prompt reporting is critical: the 2-hour internal reporting window exists specifically to give NordTech enough time to assess and, if necessary, meet the regulatory 72-hour notification deadline.

## 6. Third-Party Processors

**Section: Third-Party Processors**

NordTech shares employee personal data with the following third-party processors, each bound by a data processing agreement:

- **Personio** — HR system of record (employment data, leave records)
- **Expensify** — expense and reimbursement processing (financial data related to claims)
- **Slack** — internal communications platform
- **Google Workspace** — email, calendar, and document storage
- **Cigna** — health insurance administration (health data)

NordTech does not sell employee personal data to any third party, and does not share employee data with processors outside this list without updating this policy and notifying employees.

## 7. Scope of This Document

**Section: Scope**

This policy covers NordTech's handling of **employee** personal data specifically. It does not cover:

- Customer data handling practices (covered under NordTech's separate customer-facing privacy policy, not part of this internal document set).
- AI-specific compliance obligations under the EU AI Act (see the EU AI Act Compliance Summary).
- General data protection responsibilities in day-to-day work (see the Employee Handbook, Section 8, for a practical summary).
