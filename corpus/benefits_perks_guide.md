# NordTech Solutions — Benefits & Perks Guide

*Document type: HR / Benefits Policy*
*Source doc ID: `benefits_guide`*
*Category: Benefits*
*Country: All (with country-specific pension section)*
*Last updated: March 2026*

## 1. Health Insurance

- All employees are covered by **Cigna** health insurance from **month 1** of employment — there is no waiting period for core medical coverage.
- **Dental coverage** is included but only becomes active **after 3 months** of continuous employment.
- Coverage extends to routine care, specialist visits, and emergency treatment. Full plan details and claim forms are available via the Cigna employee portal, linked in Notion under Benefits.
- Health insurance applies uniformly across all three offices; there is no country-specific variation in the Cigna plan itself.

## 2. Pension

Pension arrangements differ by country due to local statutory requirements. Employees should refer to the section matching their country of employment.

**Section: Pension — Estonia**

- NordTech provides a **II pillar top-up of 2%** of gross salary, contributed by the employer in addition to the employee's own statutory II pillar contribution deducted via payroll.
- This top-up is on top of Estonia's mandatory state pension system and is paid directly into the employee's existing II pillar pension fund account.

**Section: Pension — Finland**

- Finnish employees participate in **TyEL** (Työntekijän eläkelaki — the Finnish statutory earnings-related pension scheme).
- TyEL contributions are mandatory under Finnish law and are handled automatically through payroll; there is no optional employer top-up beyond the statutory TyEL contribution rate.

**Section: Pension — Portugal**

- Portugal does not have a mandatory private pension top-up requirement, so NordTech offers an **optional PPR (Plano Poupança Reforma)** contribution match of **up to 3%** of gross salary.
- Employees who wish to participate must opt in via HR; the employer match is applied only up to the employee's own contribution, capped at 3%.

## 3. Gym and Wellness Budget

- All employees receive a **€50 per month** gym/wellness budget.
- Submit wellness expenses via **Expensify** (same system as travel expenses, but tagged under the "Wellness" category) and they are **reimbursed monthly**.
- The wellness budget covers gym memberships, fitness classes, and similar wellness services. It does not cover gym-adjacent retail purchases (e.g., workout clothing or equipment) — those fall outside the €50 monthly scope.

## 4. Learning Budget

- Each employee receives a **learning budget of €1,500 per year**, covering courses, books, certifications, and conferences.
- Books, online courses, and certifications up to the annual cap can be expensed directly without additional approval.
- **Conference attendance** (including associated travel) requires **manager approval** before booking, given the typically higher cost relative to the annual cap.
- The learning budget resets each calendar year and does **not** carry over unused amounts into the following year.

## 5. Home Office Setup

- **Fully remote employees** — those with an individually agreed fully remote arrangement (see the Employee Handbook, Section 7) — are eligible for a **one-time home office setup budget of €500**.
- The budget covers **furniture and ergonomic items**: a desk (including a standing desk), an office chair, a desk lamp, a footrest, or similar.
- Monitors, keyboards, headsets, and other tech peripherals are **not** part of this budget. Order those through IT's €300 peripherals budget instead (see the IT Setup Guide, Section 7).
- Hybrid employees (working up to 3 days per week from home by default) are not eligible, since they have a fully equipped office desk.
- This benefit becomes available **after 6 months** of employment, to avoid duplicating costs for employees who leave shortly after joining.
- Requests are submitted via Expensify under the "Home Office" category and require manager approval, consistent with other budget-capped benefits in this guide.

## 6. Company Laptop Refresh

- Company laptops are refreshed on a **3-year cycle**. Employees do not need to request a refresh — IT proactively schedules replacement laptops as employees approach the 3-year mark on their current device.
- Employees needing a laptop replacement sooner due to hardware failure should contact IT support directly (see IT Setup Guide, Section 6) rather than waiting for the standard refresh cycle.

## 7. Stock Options

- Employees become eligible for stock options **after 12 months** of continuous employment.
- Grant size ranges from **0.01% to 0.05%** of company equity, depending on role level and seniority.
- Stock option grants are administered by Finance and communicated individually; this guide does not cover vesting schedules or treatment upon departure — those details are provided separately in an employee's individual grant agreement.
