## 1. All Countries — Common Rules

The following rules apply to every NordTech employee regardless of office or country of employment. Country-specific entitlements and sick pay rules are covered in Sections 2–4 below, and other leave types (bereavement, unpaid leave, moving day) in Section 5.

1. All leave requests must be submitted and approved through **Personio** (our HR system). Do not request leave verbally or over Slack — it must be logged in Personio to count as approved.
2. Any leave request of **more than 5 consecutive days** must be submitted to your manager for approval **at least 2 weeks in advance**, except in cases of emergency.
3. Unused annual leave may be **carried over into the next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days**. Any unused leave beyond this cap is forfeited on 31 December unless local law requires otherwise.
4. Sick leave does not require advance approval, but employees must notify their manager as early as possible on the day of absence, ideally before the normal start of the working day.
5. All country-specific public holiday calendars are maintained in Personio and automatically applied based on your registered work location.

## 2. Estonia — Leave & Absence

**Section: Annual Leave — Estonia**

- Employees based in Estonia are entitled to **28 calendar days** of annual leave per year, in line with Estonian labour law (the statutory minimum).
- Annual leave accrues monthly and can be taken as soon as it is accrued, subject to manager approval.

**Section: Sick Leave — Estonia**

- NordTech, as the employer, pays sick leave **from day 1** of illness.
- Estonian sick pay structure: employer pays **100% of salary for the first 3 days** of illness, then **70% of salary from day 4 through day 30**. From day 31 onward, the Estonian Health Insurance Fund (Eesti Haigekassa) takes over payment via social insurance.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive working days; certificates are submitted digitally by the treating physician directly to the Estonian Health Insurance Fund and are automatically visible to NordTech's payroll system.

**Section: Public Holidays — Estonia**

NordTech observes the following Estonian public holidays:

1. New Year's Day — 1 January
2. Independence Day — 24 February
3. Good Friday (date varies)
4. Easter Sunday (date varies)
5. Spring Day — 1 May
6. Whit Sunday / Pentecost (date varies)
7. Victory Day — 23 June
8. Midsummer Day (Jaanipäev) — 24 June
9. Independence Restoration Day — 20 August
10. Christmas Eve — 24 December
11. Christmas Day — 25 December
12. Boxing Day — 26 December

**Section: Parental Leave — Estonia**

- Estonia offers one of the most generous parental leave systems in the EU. Statutory parental benefit is payable for up to **435 days**, shared between parents, at up to 100% of average income (subject to a statutory cap), administered through the Estonian Social Insurance Board rather than directly by NordTech.
- Employees should notify their manager and HR as early as possible so that Personio records and team coverage plans can be updated.

## 3. Finland — Leave & Absence

**Section: Annual Leave — Finland**

- Employees based in Finland are entitled to **25 working days** of annual leave per year. Note: this is calculated in **working days, not calendar days** — Saturdays are counted as working days under Finnish annual leave law even though most employees do not work them, so 25 working days is roughly equivalent to just over 4 calendar weeks.
- Annual leave in Finland accrues based on the "holiday credit year" (1 April – 31 March) as required under the Finnish Annual Holidays Act, though NordTech administers requests through the standard calendar-year Personio workflow for simplicity.

**Section: Sick Leave — Finland**

- Under Finnish practice, **day 1 of sick leave is unpaid**.
- NordTech, as employer, pays sick leave **from day 2** at **100% of salary for 9 calendar days** (the statutory minimum self-insurance period).
- From day 10 onward, **Kela** (the Finnish Social Insurance Institution) takes over sick pay via statutory sickness allowance.
- Employees should self-certify short absences (1–3 days) via Personio; a medical certificate is required for absences of 4 or more consecutive days.

**Section: Public Holidays — Finland**

NordTech observes the following Finnish public holidays:

1. New Year's Day — 1 January
2. Epiphany — 6 January
3. Good Friday (date varies)
4. Easter Sunday and Easter Monday (date varies)
5. May Day (Vappu) — 1 May
6. Ascension Day (date varies)
7. Midsummer's Eve and Midsummer Day (Juhannus) — late June, date varies by year
8. All Saints' Day — first Saturday of November
9. Independence Day — 6 December
10. Christmas Eve — 24 December
11. Christmas Day — 25 December
12. Boxing Day — 26 December

**Section: Parental Leave — Finland**

- Finland provides a shared parental allowance system administered by Kela: each parent is generally entitled to approximately **160 days** of parental allowance, which can be flexibly shared and partially transferred between parents.
- NordTech tops up statutory parental allowance to full salary for the first **3 months** of leave for employees with at least 12 months of tenure; beyond that, Kela's statutory allowance applies directly.

## 4. Portugal — Leave & Absence

**Section: Annual Leave — Portugal**

- Employees based in Portugal are entitled to **22 working days** of annual leave per year.
- After **3 years of continuous service**, employees receive **3 additional loyalty days**, bringing total entitlement to 25 working days. These loyalty days are automatically added to the employee's Personio balance on their 3-year work anniversary.

**Section: Sick Leave — Portugal**

- Under Portuguese law, the **first 3 days** of sick leave are unpaid by both NordTech and Segurança Social (Social Security) unless the employee has an unbroken sequence of qualifying absences in the prior 12 months.
- From **day 4 onward**, Segurança Social pays a sickness subsidy directly to the employee, ranging from **55% to 70% of reference earnings depending on the total duration of the sick leave period** (longer absences and lower prior income tend toward the higher end of the range).
- NordTech does not top up Portuguese sick pay beyond the statutory Social Security subsidy.
- A medical certificate ("Certificado de Incapacidade Temporária") is required from day 1 and must be registered by the treating physician with Segurança Social.

**Section: Public Holidays — Portugal**

NordTech observes the following Portuguese public holidays:

1. New Year's Day — 1 January
2. Good Friday (date varies)
3. Easter Sunday (date varies)
4. Freedom Day (Dia da Liberdade) — 25 April
5. Labour Day — 1 May
6. Portugal Day (Dia de Portugal) — 10 June
7. Corpus Christi (date varies)
8. Assumption of Mary — 15 August
9. Republic Day (Implantação da República) — 5 October
10. All Saints' Day — 1 November
11. Restoration of Independence — 1 December
12. Immaculate Conception — 8 December
13. Christmas Day — 25 December

**Section: Parental Leave — Portugal**

- Portugal offers "licença parental" of **120 to 150 days** depending on whether it is shared between both parents, paid via Social Security at 80–100% of reference earnings depending on the option chosen.
- An additional optional extension of up to 3 months at a reduced rate is available; employees should coordinate timing with their manager and HR well in advance.

## 5. Other Leave Types — All Countries

**Section: Bereavement Leave**

- NordTech provides at least **3 paid days** of bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (partner, child, parent, or sibling), and **1 paid day** for an extended family member (grandparent, grandchild, or parent-in-law).
- Where local law grants more bereavement leave than this (for example, in Portugal), the statutory entitlement applies instead.
- Request it in Personio as "Special" leave and let your manager know as soon as you can. No advance notice is required.

**Section: Unpaid Leave**

- Employees can take up to **30 calendar days of unpaid leave per calendar year**, with approval from both their manager and HR.
- Request unpaid leave in Personio at least **4 weeks in advance** where possible. Unpaid leave recorded after the 5th of the month is deducted in the following month's payroll (see the Working Hours & Payroll Policy).
- Cigna health insurance continues during unpaid leave. Annual leave accrual and pension contributions may pause, depending on local law.
- Unpaid leave longer than 30 days, such as a sabbatical, is assessed case by case by HR.

**Section: Moving Day**

- Employees get **1 paid moving day per calendar year** when moving home. Request it in Personio as "Special" leave.

**Section: Medical Appointments**

- Short medical or dental appointments (up to 2 hours) during working hours do not need to be booked as leave — just let your manager know. Longer absences for medical reasons are recorded as sick leave.

**Section: Leave During Probation**

- Annual leave accrues from your first day of employment and can be taken during probation, subject to manager approval like any other leave request.

## 6. How to Request Leave — Quick Reference

1. Log in to Personio.
2. Select "Request Time Off" and choose the correct leave type (Annual, Sick, Parental, Unpaid, Special).
3. For annual leave over 5 days, submit at least 2 weeks in advance.
4. For sick leave, log the absence in Personio as soon as possible and notify your manager directly via Slack or a phone call on the first day of absence.
5. Your manager receives an automatic approval request in Personio; approved leave appears on the team's shared calendar automatically.
