## Before Day 1

**Section: Before Day 1**

Responsibility for these steps sits with IT, your manager, and HR — you don't need to do anything yourself before you start, but it's useful to know what's happening behind the scenes:

1. **IT ships your laptop.** IT sends your company laptop to your home address (remote/hybrid employees) or has it ready at your office desk (see the IT Setup Guide for what happens when you first power it on).
2. **Your manager assigns a buddy.** A buddy is an existing team member who acts as your go-to person for informal questions during your first weeks — separate from your manager, who owns your formal onboarding and performance track.
3. **Your Personio account is created.** HR sets up your employee record in Personio, our HR system, which you'll use for leave requests, payroll details, and benefits enrollment from Day 1 onward.

## Day 1

**Section: Day 1**

1. **Laptop setup.** Follow the step-by-step instructions in the IT Setup Guide (Section 1: Laptop Setup) to get your device configured, including your Google Workspace account and MDM security profile.
2. **Meet your buddy.** Your buddy will reach out via Slack to introduce themselves and set up an informal first chat, typically over video call or coffee if you're in the office.
3. **Intro call with your manager.** Your manager schedules a first 1:1 on Day 1 to walk through your role, team structure, and what to expect over the coming weeks.
4. **Join your Slack channels.** You'll be added to core company-wide channels automatically (#general, #announcements, #it-support); ask your manager or buddy which team-specific channels to join beyond those.
5. **Get your office key card (office-based and hybrid employees).** Collect your key card from the Operations team at reception on Day 1 — ask for Helena Nurm (Operations Manager) in Tallinn, or the office host in Porto and Tampere. Offices are open 08:00–19:00 on working days, and the key card gives you access during those hours.

## Week 1

**Section: Week 1**

1. **Complete security training.** A short mandatory training module covering data protection, phishing awareness, and NordTech's security policies — must be completed within your first week.
2. **Set up 1Password.** Install and configure 1Password following the IT Setup Guide (Section 5: Security Requirements); this is mandatory within 24 hours of your account being created, so prioritize it early in Week 1.
3. **Read the Employee Handbook.** Review the full Handbook, particularly the sections on communication norms, remote work policy, and code of conduct, so you understand how NordTech operates day to day.
4. **Schedule 1:1s with your team.** Beyond your manager and buddy, set up short introductory 1:1s with the rest of your immediate team to understand what everyone works on.
5. **Add your bank and tax details in Personio.** Payroll needs these by the 5th of the month to pay you on the 15th — see the Working Hours & Payroll Policy for when your first salary arrives.

*If you get sick during your first week, notify your manager as early as possible (see the Leave & Absence Policy for country-specific sick leave rules) and let your buddy know so they can help cover any time-sensitive introductions or meetings you may miss.*

## 30 Days

**Section: 30 Days**

1. **Complete role-specific onboarding modules.** Depending on your team (Engineering, Design, Sales, etc.), you'll be assigned modules covering the specific tools, processes, and systems relevant to your role.
2. **First check-in with your manager.** A structured 30-day check-in to discuss how onboarding has gone so far, address any blockers, and set expectations for the next 30 days.

## 60 Days

**Section: 60 Days**

1. **First project contribution.** By 60 days, you should have made a meaningful first contribution to a live project — the scope and nature of this depends on your role and team.
2. **Feedback session with your buddy.** A more informal check-in with your buddy to reflect on how the first two months have gone and surface anything that hasn't come up in formal manager check-ins.

## 90 Days

**Section: 90 Days**

1. **Probation review meeting with your manager.** A formal review covering your performance, integration into the team, and overall fit for the role. This is NordTech's internal check-in — the legal length of your probation period is set in your employment contract and differs by country (see the Working Hours & Payroll Policy).
2. **Confirm role expectations.** Your manager will confirm ongoing role expectations and goals for the next performance cycle, aligning with NordTech's twice-yearly performance review process described in the Employee Handbook.
