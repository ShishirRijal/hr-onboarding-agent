## 1. Executive Team

NordTech Solutions is led by a three-person executive team spanning all three of the company's offices.

**Liisa Mäkinen** is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), based in the Tampere office. Liisa co-founded NordTech in 2018 and previously worked in product leadership at a Helsinki-based logistics software company. She is Finnish and holds Finnish and Estonian citizenship.

**Kaspar Tamm** is the Chief Technology Officer (CTO), based in the Tallinn office. Kaspar leads all engineering, security, and infrastructure decisions across the three engineering teams. He is Estonian and has been with NordTech since its founding.

**Ana Ferreira** is the Chief Product Officer (CPO), based in the Porto office. Ana joined NordTech in 2021 to lead the expansion of the product organization following the opening of the Porto office. She is Portuguese.

The executive team meets weekly on Mondays for a company-wide sync, and holds a quarterly in-person offsite that rotates between Tallinn, Tampere, and Porto.

## 2. Team Structure — Prose Overview

NordTech Solutions is organized into eight functional teams: Engineering, Design, Product, Sales, Marketing, HR, Finance, and Operations. Engineering is the largest function and is itself split into three sub-teams — Platform, Product Engineering, and AI/ML — each led by an Engineering Manager who reports to the CTO, Kaspar Tamm.

The **Platform team** owns core infrastructure, authentication, and the shared services layer used by the rest of the engineering organization. It is based primarily in Tallinn, with two remote engineers.

The **Product Engineering team** builds and maintains the customer-facing HR/workforce management application. This team is split roughly evenly between Tallinn and Porto.

The **AI/ML team** is the newest of the three engineering sub-teams, formed in 2024 to build NordTech's AI-assisted scheduling and recruitment-screening features. It is based mainly in Tampere and Tallinn.

Design and Product sit closely together and both report into the CPO, Ana Ferreira, from the Porto office. Sales and Marketing are led out of Tampere, reflecting NordTech's largest customer base in the Nordic region. HR, Finance, and Operations are centralized functions based in Tallinn that support all three offices.

Each team has a designated manager who is the first point of escalation for day-to-day questions, performance reviews, and expense approvals over the standard employee limit. For escalation paths beyond a direct manager, see the Employee Handbook's disciplinary and escalation process section.

## 4. How to Read This Document

- **Manager identification**: An employee's manager is the person listed as "Manager" for their team (e.g., Priit Kask is the manager for all Platform team members). For expense approvals, leave requests over 5 days, and performance reviews, the direct manager listed here is the correct point of contact.
- **Office assignment**: "Remote" next to an office name means the employee works fully remotely but is administratively attached to that office for payroll and public-holiday purposes.
- **Slack handles**: All Slack handles follow the `@firstname.lastname` convention and match the person's Google Workspace email prefix.

For questions about who to contact for a specific issue not covered by this directory (e.g., a team not listed, a recent hire not yet added), contact HR at hr@nordtech.ee.
