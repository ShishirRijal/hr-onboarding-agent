# NordTech Solutions — Expense & Reimbursement Policy

*Document type: Finance Policy*
*Source doc ID: `expense_policy`*
*Category: Finance*
*Country: All*
*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy covers business-related expenses incurred by NordTech employees while traveling, meeting clients, or working outside the standard office setup. All expenses must be submitted through **Expensify**. For IT equipment purchases (laptops, monitors, peripherals), see the IT Setup Guide instead — this policy covers travel and business expenses only.

## 2. Meal Allowance

**Section: Meal Allowance — Domestic Travel**

- Employees traveling within their home country (e.g., a Tallinn-based employee traveling to Tartu) may claim up to **€25 per day** for meals.

**Section: Meal Allowance — International Travel**

- Employees traveling internationally (e.g., to a client site, conference, or another NordTech office) may claim up to **€40 per day** for meals.
- The daily allowance covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined and is not itemized per meal.

## 3. Hotel and Accommodation

**Section: Hotel — Domestic**

- Domestic hotel stays are reimbursable up to **€150 per night**.

**Section: Hotel — International**

- International hotel stays are reimbursable up to **€200 per night**.
- Any hotel booking that exceeds these nightly caps requires **prior manager approval** before booking — retroactive approval will not be granted for over-cap bookings made without prior sign-off.

## 4. Flights

- All flights must be booked in **economy class** by default.
- **Business class** is permitted only for flights with a total duration **greater than 6 hours**, and requires **manager approval** prior to booking.
- Flights should be booked at least 2 weeks in advance where travel dates are known, to keep costs reasonable.

## 5. Receipts and Documentation

- A receipt is **required for any single expense over €25**. Expenses under €25 do not require a receipt but should still be logged with a brief description.
- Receipts must be uploaded directly into Expensify at the time of submission — photos of paper receipts are acceptable as long as the amount, date, and vendor are clearly legible.

## 6. Submission and Reimbursement Timeline

1. Submit all expenses via **Expensify within 30 days** of the expense being incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days may be rejected at manager discretion.
2. Your manager reviews and approves the expense report in Expensify.
3. Approved expenses are **reimbursed with the next salary cycle**, paid on the **15th of the month**. If an expense is approved after the cutoff for that month's payroll run, it rolls to the following month's cycle.

## 7. What Is Not Covered

The following are **not reimbursable** under this policy:

- **Alcohol**, except in the context of client entertainment, and even then capped at **€50 per event**.
- **Personal care items** (toiletries, laundry beyond multi-day trips, etc.).
- **Gym or wellness expenses** — these are covered separately under the monthly wellness budget in the Benefits & Perks Guide, not through this policy.
- **Flight or hotel upgrades** beyond what is described in Sections 3 and 4 (e.g., seat upgrades, hotel room upgrades) without prior manager approval.

Items not explicitly listed in this policy (for example, home office furniture such as a standing desk) are **not automatically covered**. If you're unsure whether something qualifies, check with your manager or HR before purchasing — do not assume reimbursement.

## 8. Client Entertainment

- Client entertainment expenses (meals, the capped alcohol allowance above) should be submitted with the client name and business purpose noted in Expensify.
- Client entertainment does not count against the personal daily meal allowance in Section 2 — it is logged as a separate expense category.
